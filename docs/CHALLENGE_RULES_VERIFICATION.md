## Re-verification 2026-09-16 (second check, same day)

Re-checked because the submission window is imminent. One material correction.

**The deadline is a cutoff, not a full launch day.** OpenAI Developers state submissions close at
**12:00am PT / 07:00 UTC on September 18, 2026**. The launch must be *scheduled* before that cutoff,
so the practical working window ends during September 17 — roughly a day earlier than a reading of
"launch on the 18th" suggests.

Prize wording is also more specific than previously recorded: the top five launches each receive
$10K in OpenAI API credits and a year of ChatGPT Pro **for up to two team members**.

The contest page rendered its countdown as `00h : 00m : 00s` when fetched without JavaScript. The
announcement page and OpenAI's own posts still describe submissions as open, so this is most likely
a hydration artifact of server-side fetching rather than a closed window. **Confirm the live
countdown in a real browser before relying on it.**

The submission guide remains a Notion page that was not fetchable in this environment. Final form
fields and eligibility remain a human pre-submit check.
